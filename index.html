What We Build

Waterfront, Done Right

From new construction to repair work, CCS builds and restores the features that define waterfront living across Summerton, Santee, Manning, and Clarendon County.

01 — Docks & Piers

Custom Docks & Piers

We build custom wood and composite docks designed around how you actually use your waterfront. Whether you want a simple place to fish or a larger layout for entertaining, we focus on smart design, strong framing, and quality materials.

02 — Seawalls

Shoreline Protection

A failing shoreline does not fix itself. If your bank is washing out or taking storm damage, a properly built seawall protects your property edge and gives you a more stable waterfront long term.

03 — Decking

Outdoor Living Spaces

We build decks that create more usable outdoor space and tie your home more naturally to the water. The goal is not just extra square footage — it is space you will actually enjoy using.

04 — Dock Repairs

Honest Repair Work

Not every project needs a full rebuild. If your dock has loose boards, storm damage, or aging components, we inspect it and give you a clear assessment before recommending the next step.
